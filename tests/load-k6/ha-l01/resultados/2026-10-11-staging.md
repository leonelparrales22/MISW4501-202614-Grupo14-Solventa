## CA-4 — gateway cache without recalculation, isolated per partner (`gateway-cache.js`)

800 customers; for each one partner A reads twice and partner B once (2 400 requests, 0.0 % failed).

- Repeated read of partner A served from the cache: **100 %** (byte-identical body).
- Stage metrics for the `GET /v1/risk-profiles/{customer_id}` method in that minute: **`CacheHitCount` 800, `CacheMissCount`
  1 600**, that is A miss + A hit + B miss for every customer. Partner B never read an entry cached for partner A.
- The script's `isolated_per_partner` rate was 99.9 % (1 of 800): it flags B when B's body is byte-identical to A's. The cache
  metrics show that B's request was a miss, so B computed its own answer and it happened to be identical (for example, the
  deterministic base profile when the sources do not answer in time). This is a false positive of the check, not a cache leak.

**Result: met** (cache metrics). The script check is a known limitation to fix.
