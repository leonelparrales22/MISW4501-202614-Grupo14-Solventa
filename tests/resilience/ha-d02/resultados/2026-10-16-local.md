**Local run, not AWS:** Docker Compose on a developer laptop (Windows 11, Docker Desktop 29.7.2), the same kind of environment
in which experiment EC-D02 was validated. It validates the mechanism (no charge lost, none duplicated, coverage only with a
confirmed payment, exceptions that can be resumed), not production latencies. The 24-hour window is shortened to 10 s only in
phase 6, as EC-D02 did.

Branch `feature/HA-D02-idempotent-premium-charge`. Command: `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 bash tests/resilience/ha-d02/run.sh`
(run `r1792203776`, 02:22:56–02:25:29 UTC on 2026-10-17, i.e. 21:22–21:25 on 2026-10-16 in Bogotá). Every phase checks with
k6 thresholds (`checks == 1`); all of them passed and the environment was removed (`down -v`).

Environment: `postgres:16-alpine`, the `payment-gateway` stub (port 9040, `SUPPORTS_IDEMPOTENCY=true`) behind
`ghcr.io/shopify/toxiproxy:2.9.0`, the `email` stub of HU-W1.3-03, `motoserver/moto:5.2.3` (local Cognito seeded by

## Counterfactual (`./run.sh counterfactual`, `SUPPORTS_IDEMPOTENCY=false`)

10 acceptances under the ambiguous answer, then the gateway restored. 3 of them were charged by the gateway while origination
waited, and after the restore every retry was charged again: **13 real charges for 10 acceptances, 3 duplicated** (k6
reports them instead of failing). It reproduces the dependency already stated by ADR-01 and decision D-10: the guarantee in
the ambiguous case requires a provider that honors the `Idempotency-Key`. It does not count to approve HA-D02.

## Observations

- **The circuit breaker limits what reaches the gateway during a fault.** With 3 failures the circuit opens for 5 s and only
  its probe is sent; the other attempts are `not_sent` and rescheduled like the ambiguous ones. That is why only 1 of the 70
  pending charges reached the gateway during phase 4 (3 of 10 in the counterfactual run). The guarantee does not depend on it:
  any charge that did reach the gateway is replayed with the same key.
- **The retries of an outage synchronize.** All the pending charges reach the 60-second cap and are attempted in the same
  batch, ordered by `next_attempt_at`; with the circuit open, the probe is usually the same charge in every cycle. After the
  restore the first probe succeeds, the circuit closes and the backlog converges within one cycle (9–21 s in the local runs,
  at most about 60 s plus the 5 s of the circuit). Adding jitter to the waits would spread them; that is a parameter for the
  team to decide (the INVEST review keeps the policy negotiable), not part of this story.
- The Go HTTP transport may send a request again on a new connection when a reused one is cut before the answer, because the
  request carries an `Idempotency-Key`; the stub counts it as another request with the same key, never as another charge.
